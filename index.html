The Daily Deal

Everyone gets the same deck today.

The Daily Deal is one game a day. Every player in the world gets the same shuffle on the same date. You play it once. When you finish, the game shows you the opening deck, all 52 cards in order, so you can check that the deal wasn't stacked against you.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no trial and no timer. The free games never run out.

Do I need to make an account?

No. Open the link and you're playing.

Can the family play on one phone?

Yes. Pass-and-play is in this test build for up to 4 players.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once it has loaded once. Add it to your home screen, then try it in airplane mode.

Is the shuffle fair?

Every draw comes from a real 52-card deck, and the raid chance counts what's actually left. After the Daily Deal you can see the opening deck, all 52 cards in order, for yourself.
